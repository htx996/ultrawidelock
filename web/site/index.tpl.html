Distance is measured,
not trusted.

Signal strength can be faked from across the street. Time of flight cannot: light takes 3.34 ns per metre, and nothing makes it arrive early. Drag the phone, then arm a relay.

A relay can add delay and nothing else, so arming one pushes the measurement away from the door. One tick of the radio’s clock is 4.692 mm of light travel, and every reading here is computed from the firmware’s own constants. How it is measured

The phone already
in your pocket.

An iPhone or Apple Watch opens it on approach over UWB or on tap over NFC, and the lock reports its state to Apple Home over Matter.

Learned from the air.
Written by hand.

Five steps in the order they happen, each one studied as it crossed the air and reimplemented in portable C: the credential exchange, the key ladder, STS, DS-TWR, and a Matter node without CHIP. The notes are public.

Protocol notes The on-air study
  1. BLEThe phone finds the lock’s credential service.
  2. AuthenticateAUTH0, AUTH1 and EXCHANGE, and both ends hold the ranging key.
  3. RangeThe key ladder seeds an STS session, then one DS-TWR round.
  4. GateA trusted range inside 1.00 m moves the bolt.
  5. HomeLock state into Apple Home over Thread or Wi-Fi.

Three chip families.
One portable core.

  • DWM3001CDK nRF52833 + DW3110

    The reference board. Radio, MCU and a J-Link on one part, nothing to wire. Matter over Thread. Also builds without Zephyr.

  • nRF5340 DK DWM3000EVB + NFC12A1

    UWB and NFC on the same lock, so a tap works when a walk-up does not. Matter over Thread.

  • ESP32-S3 / C5 / C6 DWM3000EVB

    Matter over Wi-Fi, and the tier the web flasher installs with no toolchain at all.

Clone to a passing suite
in one minute.

A C compiler and python3 are the whole requirement. No board, no toolchain, and nothing phones home.

shellno hardware
git clone https://github.com/ultrawidelock/ultrawidelock.git
cd ultrawidelock
make check                  # 18 suites, 9,608 checks
shellDWM3001CDK
make dfu-key                # once per clone   · image-signing key
make bootstrap              # once per machine · NCS v3.3.0
make build                  # -> build/cdk-matter
make flash                  # over the on-board J-Link
make monitor                # console, over RTT
